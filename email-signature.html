African Sky Resources email signature

Fill in your details, check the preview, then copy it into your email app. One signature per person.

Installing it

  1. GoDaddy Professional Email (webmail): Settings → Signature → paste → Save.
  2. Microsoft 365 / Outlook on the web: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature → paste.
  3. Outlook desktop: File → Options → Mail → Signatures → New → paste.
  4. Phone apps: most only take plain text. Use name, title, company, mobile and website.

The banner rotates through three projects: Mufumbwe gold, Mineral Partners copper and mobile processing. Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook on the web and phones play the animation; Outlook desktop on Windows shows only the first slide. Clicking the banner opens the Projects section of the website.

The logo loads from https://www.africanskyresources.com/assets/email/asr-logo-tile.png, so it only shows in sent mail once the website is live on that domain. Until then, recipients see the text signature with an empty logo space.
